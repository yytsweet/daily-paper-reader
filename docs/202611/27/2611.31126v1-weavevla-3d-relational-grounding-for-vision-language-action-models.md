## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型利用预训练的视觉-语言表示来实现指令条件下的机器人控制，但其对任务相关三维关系的理解以及这些关系在操作过程中如何演化，在很大程度上仍是隐式的。为应对这一挑战，我们提出 WeaveVLA，该框架通过显式建模任务相关的相对三维关系，并在多次观测之间保持关系上下文，来增强 VLA 的动作生成。WeaveVLA 采用任务条件化的稀疏图神经网络，对物体、夹爪及邻近参考物之间的相对三维关系进行编码。成对特定的循环边状态在不同观测之间整合几何证据，以维持不断演化的空间上下文。一种基于可学习查询的读出机制将更新后的图表示汇总为紧凑的关系 token，与原始视觉-语言上下文一起对动作专家进行条件化。辅助的几何重建目标进一步促进空间结构的保持。我们将 WeaveVLA 实例化于 StarVLA-GR00T 和 π_{0.5} 之上，两种骨干网络在 LIBERO 上均取得超过 98% 的平均成功率。在 LIBERO-Plus 上，WeaveVLA 分别将成功率从 74.9% 提升至 82.0%、从 85.7% 提升至 87.8%，并在 CALVIN D→D 上取得进一步提升。在三个真实世界任务上，基于 StarVLA-GR00T 的变体在标准条件下将平均成功率从 55.6% 提升至 70.0%，在分布外（OOD）扰动下从 33.3% 提升至 53.3%，在两种设置下均超过 π_{0.5} 基线。

## Abstract
Vision-language-action (VLA) models leverage pretrained vision-language representations for instruction-conditioned robot control, yet their understanding of task-relevant 3D relations and how these relations evolve during manipulation remains largely implicit. To address this challenge, we introduce \textbf{WeaveVLA}, a framework that enhances VLA action generation by explicitly modeling task-relevant relative 3D relations and maintaining relational context across observations. WeaveVLA employs a task-conditioned sparse graph neural network to encode relative 3D relations among objects, the gripper, and nearby references. Pair-specific recurrent edge states integrate geometric evidence across observations to maintain evolving spatial context. A learned-query readout summarizes the updated graph representations into compact relational tokens, which condition the action expert alongside the original vision-language context. An auxiliary geometry reconstruction objective further encourages spatial structure preservation. We instantiate WeaveVLA on StarVLA-GR00T and $π_{0.5}$, achieving over 98\% average success on LIBERO with both backbones. On LIBERO-Plus, WeaveVLA improves success from 74.9\% to 82.0\% and from 85.7\% to 87.8\%, respectively, with additional improvements on CALVIN D$\rightarrow$D. On three real-world tasks, the StarVLA-GR00T-based variant improves average success from 55.6\% to 70.0\% under standard conditions and from 33.3\% to 53.3\% under OOD perturbations, surpassing the $π_{0.5}$ baseline in both settings.
