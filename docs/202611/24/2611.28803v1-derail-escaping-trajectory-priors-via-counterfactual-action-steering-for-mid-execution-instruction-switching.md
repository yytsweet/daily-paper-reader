## 摘要
在真实世界中运行的机器人常常需要在执行过程中用户指令发生变化时做出适应，然而当前以指令为条件的机器人策略（包括视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型和世界-动作模型（WAM））大多在固定指令的回合设置下进行评估。我们研究执行中指令切换问题，并识别出轨迹劫持这一关键失败模式：策略未能充分转向切换后的目标，其切换后的行为仍完全或部分被拉向与非目标任务相关的轨迹。我们提出反事实动作引导（Counterfactual Action Steering, CAS），这是一种无需训练、与模型无关的推理时方法，它识别造成干扰的非目标任务，用相应的反事实指令查询同一策略，并利用目标与反事实动作之间的差异来引导执行远离干扰性轨迹先验。在五个 VLA 和 WAM 策略上于 LIBERO-Goal-Switch 和 CALVIN-Custom-Switch 进行的实验，以及真实机器人评估表明，CAS 能持续提升切换成功率；例如，在 CALVIN-Custom-Switch 的 $[15,21]$ 切换区间内，CAS 将 $\pi_{0.5}$ 从 5.8% 提升至 54.6%。成功的 CAS 引导轨迹还能在相同轨迹数量下提供比普通成功轨迹更高质量的自训练监督，从而得到更强的策略，并可泛化到未见过的后续指令切换。代码将公开。

## Abstract
Robots operating in the real world must often adapt when user instructions change mid-execution, yet current instruction-conditioned robot policies, including Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and World-Action Models (WAMs), are largely evaluated under fixed-instruction episodes. We study mid-execution instruction switching and identify trajectory hijacking as a key failure mode, where the policy fails to redirect sufficiently toward the switched target, with its post-switch behavior remaining fully or partially drawn toward trajectories associated with non-target tasks. We propose Counterfactual Action Steering (CAS), a training-free, model-agnostic inference-time method that identifies interfering non-target tasks, queries the same policy with corresponding counterfactual instructions, and uses target--counterfactual action differences to steer execution away from interfering trajectory priors. Experiments on LIBERO-Goal-Switch and CALVIN-Custom-Switch across five VLA and WAM policies, together with real-robot evaluation, show that CAS consistently improves switching success; for example, on CALVIN-Custom-Switch in the $[15,21]$ switching range, CAS improves $π_{0.5}$ from 5.8\% to 54.6\%. Successful CAS-guided rollouts further provide higher-quality self-training supervision than vanilla successful rollouts with the same number of trajectories, yielding stronger policies that generalize to unseen later instruction switches. Code will be made publicly available.
