## 摘要
模型预测路径积分（MPPI）控制是基于模型的强化学习（MBRL）在线规划的核心，但其迭代细化会带来规划延迟。减少细化迭代次数通常会降低性能，这促使从规划器中学习更强的策略引导。然而，现有方法通常通过动作级对齐从规划器学习，无法直接捕捉完整的 H 步规划器提议。此外，规划器模仿将进一步的目标改进留给在线细化。为解决这些局限，我们提出 FaSt World PlANner（SWAN），一种优势感知的基于 MPPI 的 MBRL 框架，显式表示并改进完整的 H 步规划器提议，以改进策略引导，实现高效在线规划。在提议表示方面，SWAN 将 k-MPPI（一种多模态 MPPI 扩展）与混合因子分析器（MFA）动作块策略相结合，以捕捉多个模式以及跨时间和动作维度的相关性。在提议优化方面，我们引入优势引导的提议优化（Advantage-Guided Proposal Optimization, APO），它复用 MPPI 评估，利用优势估计构建改进目标，并在保持规划器对齐的同时将策略训练至该目标。在来自 DMControl、HumanoidBench 和 MyoSuite 的 31 个高维连续控制任务上的实验表明，与最先进的基于规划的 MBRL 方法相比，SWAN 在使用少 50% 的 MPPI 细化迭代次数的同时，显著提高了控制性能和样本效率。最后，SWAN 在物理 Unitree Go2 上展示了零样本仿真到现实迁移，实现了高频控制下稳定的速度跟踪运动。（演示见 https://swanfastworldplanner.github.io/）

## Abstract
Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) control is central to online planning for model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL), but its iterative refinement incurs planning latency. Reducing refinement iterations often degrades performance, motivating stronger policy guidance learned from the planner. However, existing methods typically learn from the planner through action-level alignment, which does not directly capture the complete $H$-step planner proposal. Moreover, planner imitation leaves further target improvement to online refinement. To address these limitations, we propose FaSt World PlANner (SWAN), an advantage-aware MPPI-based MBRL framework that explicitly represents and improves the complete $H$-step planner proposal to improve policy guidance for efficient online planning. For proposal representation, SWAN combines k-MPPI, a multimodal MPPI extension, with a mixture of factor analyzers (MFA) action-chunk policy to capture multiple modes and correlations across time and action dimensions. For proposal optimization, we introduce Advantage-Guided Proposal Optimization (APO), which reuses MPPI evaluations to construct an improved target using advantage estimates and trains the policy toward this target while preserving planner alignment. Experiments across 31 high-dimensional continuous-control tasks from DMControl, HumanoidBench, and MyoSuite show that SWAN substantially improves control performance and sample efficiency while using 50% fewer MPPI refinement iterations than state-of-the-art planning-based MBRL methods. Finally, SWAN demonstrates zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a physical Unitree Go2, achieving stable velocity-tracking locomotion with high-frequency control. (see our demo at https://swanfastworldplanner.github.io/)
