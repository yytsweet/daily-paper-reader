## 摘要
学习到的操作策略，从任务特定的模仿策略到视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型，主要依赖 RGB 图像。尽管一些研究报告了传感器深度带来的收益，另一些研究则因测量噪声而避免使用深度相机。深度相机会同时引入噪声和缺失的深度值（空洞），二者的相对影响仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们使用真实的腕部安装立体相机校准传感器模型，并在仿真中分别评估噪声和空洞对采用三种视觉编码器的 RGB-D 模仿策略的影响。我们发现，完整的深度可将成功率提高约十个百分点，而且即使噪声达到相机噪声水平的二十倍，大部分增益仍然存在。相比之下，覆盖约六分之一腕部图像的空洞会消除可检测到的深度收益。有效性掩码会强烈影响对深度的依赖：将一小部分像素标记为无效会降低这种依赖，而填补空洞并将其标记为有效则可恢复大部分收益。在干净深度上训练的策略，当空洞仅在部署时出现时也会损失性能，有时甚至低于仅使用 RGB 的性能。因此，这些发现表明，对于学习型策略而言，评判深度相机应更多依据深度覆盖度，而非测量精度。

## Abstract
Learned manipulation policies, from task-specific imitation policies to vision-language-action (VLA) models, rely primarily on RGB images. While some studies report gains from sensor depth, others avoid depth cameras because of measurement noise. Depth cameras introduce both noise and missing depth values (holes), whose relative effects remain unclear. In this study, we calibrate a sensor model using a real wrist-mounted stereo camera and separately evaluate noise and holes on RGB-D imitation policies with three visual encoders in simulation. We found that complete depth improves success by approximately ten percentage points, and most of this gain persists even at twenty times the camera's noise level. In contrast, holes covering about one-sixth of the wrist image eliminate the detectable benefit of depth. The validity mask strongly influences depth reliance: marking a small fraction of pixels as invalid reduces reliance, while filling holes and marking them valid restores most of the benefit. Policies trained on clean depth also lose performance when holes appear only at deployment, sometimes falling below RGB-only performance. These findings suggest that depth cameras for learned policies should therefore be judged by depth coverage more than measurement precision.
