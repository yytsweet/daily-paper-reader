## 摘要
力与触觉反馈能够提供接触密集操作中物理交互的关键信息，而仅凭视觉观测无法可靠地捕获这些信息。然而，由于视觉-语言语义与物理测量之间存在表征差距，将这些信号集成到视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型中仍然具有挑战性。为解决这一挑战，我们提出了 A2I-VLA，这是一个两阶段 VLA 框架：首先学习语义对齐的力-触觉表征，然后将其集成到 VLA 模型中。在第一阶段，引入跨模态对齐损失以促进语义表征与物理表征之间的对应关系。随后，通过分层多模态融合网络将触觉与力特征流进行集成，并通过未来力旋量预测损失和动作预测损失对所得表征进行联合监督。前者鼓励模型预测物理响应，后者将力-触觉观测与任务相关的操作行为关联起来。在第二阶段，门控交叉注意力桥接模块将第一阶段学习到的分层物理特征自适应地集成到 VLA 模型中，以指导动作生成。真实世界实验表明，所提出的 A2I-VLA 在三个接触密集操作任务上显著优于基线 PI05，将成功率从 70% 提高到 95%。

## Abstract
Force and tactile feedback provide essential information about physical interactions in contact-rich manipulation that visual observations alone cannot reliably capture.However, integrating these signals into vision-language-action (VLA) models remains challenging owing to the representational gap between visual-language semantics and physical measurements.To address this challenge, we propose A2I-VLA, a two-stage VLA framework that first learns semantically Aligned force-tactile representations and then Integrates them into a VLA model.In Stage I, a cross-modal alignment loss is introduced to promote correspondence between semantic and physical representations. The tactile and force feature streams are then integrated through a hierarchical multimodal fusion network, and the resulting representations are jointly supervised by future wrench and action prediction losses. The former encourages the model to anticipate physical responses, while the latter associates force-tactile observations with task-relevant manipulation behaviors.In Stage II, a gated cross-attention bridge module adaptively integrates the hierarchical physical features learned in Stage I into the VLA model to guide action generation.Real-world experiments demonstrate that the proposed A2I-VLA significantly outperforms the baseline PI05 across three contact-rich manipulation tasks, improving the success rate from 70% to 95%.
