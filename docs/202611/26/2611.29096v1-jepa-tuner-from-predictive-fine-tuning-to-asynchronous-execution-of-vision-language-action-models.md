## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型根据视觉观测、本体感觉状态和语言指令生成动作序列。在具身交互中，执行这些动作会影响后续观测和机器人状态，从而为策略学习提供自然的预测信号。然而，标准的动作监督微调并未在动作预测器所消费的引导中显式捕捉这些以动作为条件的时间依赖关系。我们提出 JEPA-Tuner，一种预测式微调框架，通过以动作为条件的未来预测对 VLA 的原生策略引导进行正则化。在微调过程中，JEPA-Tuner 中的预测器学习在给定当前引导和已执行动作的条件下，匹配由未来观测编码得到的引导。该目标促使跨模态引导适配器捕捉场景和机器人状态在这些动作下如何演化，同时基于观测引导和预测引导联合训练动作专家，使表示保持对控制有益的信息。此外，当以异步动作块推理部署 VLA 模型时，预训练的预测器将预测下一个块边界处所需的引导。因此，策略可以提前生成下一个动作块，并从其第一个动作开始执行，而无需重叠校正或额外的预测器训练，从而实现高效且高质量的异步执行。在 LIBERO 和 Meta-World 上的实验表明，JEPA-Tuner 在同步推理下借助更好的多模态引导嵌入提升了任务成功率。对于异步控制，在 LIBERO 仿真器和多尺寸真实机器人演示中，JEPA-Tuner 在不同延迟设置下均提升了性能和平滑性。包含视频演示的项目网站为 https://qujx.github.io/JEPA-Tuner-Website。

## Abstract
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models generate sequences of actions from visual observations, proprioceptive states, and language instructions. In embodied interaction, executing these actions influences subsequent observations and robot states, providing a natural predictive signal for policy learning. However, standard action-supervised fine-tuning does not explicitly capture these action-conditioned temporal dependencies in the guidance consumed by the action predictor. We introduce JEPA-Tuner, a predictive fine-tuning framework that regularizes the VLA's native policy guidance through action-conditioned future prediction. During fine-tuning, a predictor in JEPA-Tuner learns to match guidance encoded from a future observation given the current guidance and committed actions. This objective encourages a cross-modal guidance adapter to capture how the scene and robot state evolve under those actions, while jointly training the action expert on both observed and predicted guidance keeps the representation informative for control. In addition, when deploying the VLA model with asynchronous action-chunk inference, the pre-trained predictor will anticipate the guidance needed at the next chunk boundary. The policy can thus generate the next chunk in advance and execute it from its first action without overlap correction or additional predictor training, enabling efficient and high-quality asynchronous execution. Experiments on LIBERO and Meta-World show that JEPA-Tuner improves task success under synchronous inference with better multi-modal guidance embeddings. For asynchronous control, JEPA-Tuner improves performance and smoothness across different latency settings on both LIBERO simulator and multi-size real robot demonstrations.
