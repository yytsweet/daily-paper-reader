## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型受益于强语义表征，这些表征支持物体识别和指令理解。然而，精确操作还依赖于解析物体位置与工作空间关系，并依赖于在估计场景将如何演化的基础上选择动作。现有几何感知方法使当前场景更加显式，而预测方法则对未来观测或表征进行建模。然而，未来信息往往既未接地于控制相关的空间状态，也未直接用于指导动作生成。我们提出空间接地潜在前瞻（SG-LL），其以接地的当前状态为参考，预测可操作的近未来表征。一个冻结的空间教师首先将几何先验蒸馏到与策略连接的空间令牌中。随后，一个以当前状态为条件的瓶颈学习紧凑潜在表示，以捕获相对于当前场景的未来信息。该潜在表示由当前策略上下文和初始动作专家特征预测得到，无需访问未来观测或空间教师。最后，零初始化残差融合将预测的潜在表示引入动作专家，以细化动作生成。在 LIBERO-Plus、CALVIN 和三个真实世界操作任务上的实验表明，在仿真和真实机器人设置中均取得了改进。

## Abstract
Vision-language-action (VLA) models benefit from strong semantic representations that support object recognition and instruction understanding. However, precise manipulation also depends on resolving object locations and workspace relations, and on selecting actions with an estimate of how the scene will evolve. Existing geometry-aware methods make the current scene more explicit, while predictive methods model future observations or representations. Yet future information is often neither grounded in a control-relevant spatial state nor directly used to guide action generation. We introduce Spatially Grounded Latent Lookahead (SG-LL), which uses the grounded present as the reference for predicting an actionable near-future representation. A frozen spatial teacher first distills geometric priors into policy-connected spatial tokens. A current-conditioned bottleneck then learns a compact latent that captures future information relative to the present scene. This latent is predicted from the current policy context and initial Action Expert features, without access to future observations or the spatial teacher. Finally, zero-initialized residual fusion incorporates the predicted latent into the Action Expert to refine action generation.
